Manage the user's emails via IMAP/SMTP. Actions: check (list inbox, unread=true lists only the unread ones), read (full email by msg_id, several separated by commas), attachment (text of an attachment: PDF, Office, text), search (by subject/sender), send, list_folders, mark (read/unread/flagged/unflagged). Deleting, moving and creating folders are NOT here — they are in email_manage and only work from the web UI.
Long mails and attachments are read by lines like a file: the answer names the lines it holds, go on with line_start.
Lists show date, sender and subject, no content: to know what mails say, read them, several at once in one call. In lists, 📩 marks an unread mail and 📧 a read one.

CRITICAL: every email operation needs a tool call — writing text sends or reads nothing. NEVER say an email was sent unless a tool_result confirms it.

SENDING — MANDATORY, EVERY SINGLE TIME:
1) Show the draft first (To, Subject, Body) and ask for confirmation — even right after a previous email, even for "send another one". Always give a meaningful subject line.
2) After explicit confirmation ("ja", "abschicken", "send it") call email with action=send immediately — do not repeat the draft, write a new one or ask again.

The body is Markdown (rendered to HTML). Sending to a recipient who is not on the allowlist only works from the web UI; from an external channel it is refused.
