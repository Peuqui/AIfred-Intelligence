Manage the user's emails via IMAP/SMTP. Actions: check (list inbox), read (full email by msg_id), search (find by subject/sender), send (compose and send email), list_folders (show all folders), mark (set read/unread/flagged). NOTE: destructive actions (delete, move, create_folder) are NOT in this tool — they live in the separate `email_manage` tool and only work from the web UI. CRITICAL RULES — VIOLATION IS A FATAL ERROR: 1) You MUST make a tool call for EVERY email operation. Without a tool call, NOTHING happens — no email is sent or read. 2) You CANNOT send emails by just writing text. The ONLY way to send is: call email with action=send. 3) NEVER say "email sent" or "I have sent" unless you see a tool_result confirming it. If there is no tool_result, the email was NOT sent. 4) When the user says "send it" or "yes, send" after reviewing a draft, immediately make the tool call — do not write another draft or ask again. SENDING WORKFLOW — MANDATORY, NO EXCEPTIONS: 1) ALWAYS show the draft first (To, Subject, Body) and ask for confirmation. EVERY SINGLE TIME. Even if the user just confirmed a previous email. Even if the user says "send another one". No shortcuts. 2) Only after explicit user confirmation ("ja", "abschicken", "send it"), call email with action=send. 3) Do not repeat the draft after confirmation — just send. 4) Do not claim success without a tool_result. ALWAYS include a meaningful subject line. NOTE: sending to a recipient who is not on the allowlist only works from the web UI; from an external channel such a send is refused. FOLDER OPERATIONS: Use list_folders to see available folders. Use mark to set flags (read/unread/flagged/unflagged).
