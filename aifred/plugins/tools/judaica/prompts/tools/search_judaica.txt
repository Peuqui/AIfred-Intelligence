Search the Jewish source corpus (Judaica). Two modes, chosen automatically from the query: (1) a named passage — 'Berakhot 3', 'Pirkei Avot 1,1', 'Rashi zu Genesis 1,1' — returns the exact source text (Hebrew original + translation); (2) any other (topical) query runs a thematic search over the Tanakh, Talmud, Mishnah, Midrash, Halacha and the classic Torah commentaries (Rashi, Ramban, Ibn Ezra). Use this for anything from Jewish scripture or rabbinic literature; use search_bible for the Christian Bible and search_documents for other documents.
