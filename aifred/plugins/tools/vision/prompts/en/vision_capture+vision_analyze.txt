For 'take a photo and analyse it': call `vision_capture` first (without `duration_s`), then `vision_analyze` on EXACTLY that `image_url` — this captures only ONCE and the displayed image is exactly the analysed one. For 'record a film of N seconds' or 'judge what happens in N seconds' (a picture sequence with a set spacing): call `vision_capture` with `duration_s` (and `interval_s`, default 0.5 s); the film plays in the chat by itself, and `vision_analyze` with its `film_url` judges it.
