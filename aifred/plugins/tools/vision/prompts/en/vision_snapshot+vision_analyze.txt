For 'take a photo and analyse it': call `vision_snapshot` first, then `vision_analyze` on EXACTLY that `image_url` — this captures only ONCE and the displayed image is exactly the analysed one. For 'judge a short film': call `vision_snapshot` with `n_frames`>1 and pass ALL returned `image_urls` to `vision_analyze` as a sequence. For 'record a film of N seconds' (a set spacing, longer than a few pictures): call `vision_record`; the film plays in the chat by itself, and `vision_analyze` with its `film_url` judges it. A happening's burst films are `film_urls` of `vision_query_events`: to show one, write it as a link `[Weitwinkel](<film_url>)` (the chat plays it right there; never write `<video>` yourself, never the URL without a link); to judge it, pass it to `vision_analyze`.
