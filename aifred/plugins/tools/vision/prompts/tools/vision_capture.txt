Take a picture or record a film of a source, into the current session — does NOT analyse. Without `duration_s` (the default) it takes ONE photo; with `duration_s` it records a FILM: one picture every `interval_s` seconds for `duration_s` seconds, assembled in real time (not sped up) into an MP4 — for 'record a short film', 'watch the door for 30 seconds', a picture sequence with a set spacing. The call takes as long as the recording. Limits: `duration_s` at most the configured maximum, `interval_s` at least the configured minimum — a value outside them is answered with the limit. Returns for a photo `image_url` and `source_id`, for a film `film_url`, `n_frames`, `duration_s` and `fps`. In the web chat the photo or film is shown automatically — do NOT echo it; on a chat channel (Telegram, Discord) a reply carries text only, so send it with the channel's send tool, the URL as the attachment. To JUDGE what is on a photo or happens in a film, pass its URL to `vision_analyze`.
