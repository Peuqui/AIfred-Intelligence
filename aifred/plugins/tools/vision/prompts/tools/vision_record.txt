Record a FILM from a source: one picture every `interval_s` seconds for `duration_s` seconds, assembled into an MP4 that plays in real time (not sped up). Use it for 'record a short film', 'watch the door for 30 seconds', a picture sequence with a set spacing; for a quick burst of a few pictures use `vision_snapshot` with `n_frames`. The call takes as long as the recording. Limits: `duration_s` at most the configured maximum and `interval_s` at least the configured minimum — a value outside them is answered with the limit. Returns `film_url`, `n_frames`, `duration_s` and `fps`; in the web chat the film plays by itself — do NOT echo it; on a chat channel (Telegram, Discord) a reply carries text only, so send the film with the channel's send tool, `film_url` as the attachment. To JUDGE what happens in the film, pass `film_url` to `vision_analyze`.
