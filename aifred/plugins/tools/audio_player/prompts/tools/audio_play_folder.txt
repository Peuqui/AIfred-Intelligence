Play ALL audio files below a folder (recursive, sub-folders included) one after another in natural order ('CD 1' < 'CD 2' < 'CD 10') — for audiobooks with several parts or albums. 'folder' is label-prefixed: 'hoerbuecher' for the whole source, 'hoerbuecher/Tolkien_HdR' for a sub-folder. shuffle=true for random order. Works on browser and FreeEcho.2 targets, not yet on the local channel.
