Play ALL audio files in a folder sequentially in natural alphabetical order (e.g. 'CD 1' < 'CD 2' < 'CD 10'). Use for audiobooks with multiple parts or albums. The 'folder' parameter is a label-prefixed path: 'hoerbuecher' for the entire source, or 'hoerbuecher/Tolkien_HdR' for a sub-folder. Resolution is recursive — all audio files below the folder (including sub-folders) are queued. Set 'shuffle=True' for random order. Works on browser and FreeEcho.2 targets; local channel not yet.
