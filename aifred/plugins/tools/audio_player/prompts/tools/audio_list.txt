List configured audio sources (when 'source' is omitted) or items in a specific source folder (with optional 'subdir' to scope deeper). Uses the SQLite index when populated (fast, scales to 100k+ files); falls back to filesystem walk if the source isn't indexed yet. DOES NOT PLAY ANYTHING — only for discovery. To start playback, call audio_play(item='label/file.mp3') after finding the right item. For large sources, prefer audio_search() over listing everything.
