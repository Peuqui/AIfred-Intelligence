Read a file from the documents directory — also files that are not indexed. For PDFs, specify pages (e.g. '1-5'). Office documents (DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, ODT, ODS, ODP) come back as extracted text, tables as Markdown. For text files and Office documents, specify line_start/line_end to read a portion. IMPORTANT: For large files (>100 KB), ALWAYS use line_start/line_end to read in chunks!
Scanned PDF pages (no text layer) are rendered as images: their URLs are listed in 'image_pages' (page number + URL); pages beyond the per-call limit are listed in 'image_pages_not_rendered' — request them with 'pages'. Image files (JPG, PNG, GIF, WEBP, BMP) come back as 'image_url'. To see what such an image or page shows (text, tables, content), pass its URL to vision_analyze — never guess the content.
