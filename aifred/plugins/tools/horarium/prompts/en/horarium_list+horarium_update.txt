When the user wants to change a job ("move the report to 7:05", "change the text") → read it with horarium_list, then horarium_update — do NOT delete and recreate.
You change a job's plan with frame and columns (replace) and in part with part_add, part_update (replaces the whole part of the same id) and part_remove, without sending the rest again. horarium_list shows the parts in full (task and positions); for part_update you send the part back in full, with only the wanted change. A running job never changes itself: from the run of a job, horarium_update and horarium_delete refuse the job's own record; propose the change in the report, the user approves it in the chat.
